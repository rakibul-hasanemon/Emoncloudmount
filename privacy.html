Privacy Policy

Emoncloudmount · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Scope: Emoncloudmount is a personal project that uses rclone on the project owner's computer. This policy describes that personal setup; update it if the project or its data practices change.

1. Information collected by Emoncloudmount

The project owner does not operate a separate Emoncloudmount server and does not intentionally collect or maintain other people's Google Drive files, passwords, or personal information through this project. The project is intended for the owner's personal use.

2. Google Drive access and OAuth

When rclone is authorized to access Google Drive, Google may provide an authorization token to rclone. rclone stores its configuration and authorization information locally according to its configuration. The permissions available to rclone depend on the scopes and options selected during setup. Google processes authorization requests and Drive data under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

The computer's owner is responsible for securing the computer, the rclone configuration file, backups, and any credentials stored on that computer. Anyone with access to those credentials may be able to access the connected account within the granted permissions.

3. File transfers

Files accessed or transferred through rclone are handled according to the commands and configuration chosen by the computer's owner. This project does not independently upload file contents to an Emoncloudmount-operated server.

4. Website hosting

This informational website may be hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as server logs and request information, to provide and protect its hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement for details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Third-party services

This project relies on third-party services and software, including Google Drive, Google OAuth, and rclone. Their terms, security practices, and privacy policies apply to their respective services. Review Google's privacy policy at Google Privacy Policy and rclone's documentation at rclone.org.

6. Data retention and deletion

The project owner does not maintain a separate Emoncloudmount database of users' files. To revoke rclone's access, remove the app's access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings and remove the relevant local rclone remote or configuration as appropriate. Removing local configuration does not itself delete files stored in Google Drive.

7. Children's privacy

This personal project is not designed as a service for children and is not offered to the public as a user account service.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the project or its practices change. The date at the top indicates when this page was last updated.
